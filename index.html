🚨 Top 2 Critical Structural Issues Requiring Ministerial Reform

Unanimously identified by ground Public Health Medicine Specialists & DHO leadership as the most urgent structural bottlenecks.

#1 TOP PRIORITY REFORM

THREAT-MOH — Top-Down Ministerial Policy & Implementation Disconnect

Mentioned by N = 31 / 37 PKDs (84% of Respondents)

"Mismatch between central ministry planners and ground operational realities, where new programs are introduced without taking into account staffing constraints and current workload at district level."

— Sarawak (17 years of service)

"Dasar dan program baharu dari peringkat kementerian sering diturunkan secara mendadak tanpa peruntukan sumber manusia atau logistik tambahan yang mencukupi untuk daerah."

— Terengganu (15 years of service)

"Peringkat tertinggi kerap menukar arahan dan KPI secara ad-hoc tanpa memahami cabaran geografi dan akses fasiliti di kawasan pedalaman."

— Sabah (12 years of service)
🔧 Ministerial Reform Action: Mandate ground feasibility impact assessments and district co-design prior to any central policy rollout!
#2 SECOND PRIORITY REFORM

WEAK-HR-TASK — Multi-Tasking & Concurrent Event Overload

Mentioned by N = 33 / 37 PKDs (89% of Respondents)

"Staff are forced to manage routine clinical duties while simultaneously conducting outbreak control, disaster response, and official events, leading to severe staff exhaustion."

— Terengganu (15 years of service)

"Petugas yang sama perlu menjaga pesakit di klinik, keluar melakukan semburan fogging Denggi, dan bertugas di pusat pemindahan banjir pada minggu yang sama."

— Sabah (12 years of service)

"Keperluan menghadiri mesyuarat dan aktiviti pengumpulan data yang bertindih menjejaskan masa perkhidmatan terus kepada pesakit di klinik."

— Pahang (14 years of service)
🔧 Ministerial Reform Action: Establish dedicated rapid-response surge teams to protect routine primary care delivery!
