🚨 Top 2 Critical Structural Issues Requiring Ministerial Reform

Unanimously identified by ground Public Health Medicine Specialists & DHO leadership as the most urgent structural bottlenecks.

#1 TOP PRIORITY REFORM

THREAT-MOH — Top-Down Ministerial Policy & Implementation Disconnect

Mentioned by N = 30 / 36 PKDs (83% of Respondents)

"Ketidakselarian antara perancang di peringkat kementerian dengan realiti operasi di lapangan, di mana program baharu sering diperkenalkan tanpa mengambil kira kekangan perjawatan dan beban kerja semasa di daerah."

— Sarawak (17 years of service)
🔧 Ministerial Reform Action: Mandate ground feasibility impact assessments and district co-design prior to any central policy rollout!
#2 SECOND PRIORITY REFORM

WEAK-HR-TASK — Multi-Tasking & Concurrent Event Overload

Mentioned by N = 32 / 36 PKDs (89% of Respondents)

"Petugas perlu menjalankan tugas rutin di klinik serta tugasan kawalan wabak dan bencana serentak, menyebabkan kelesuan anggota dan risiko bebanan kerja lampau."

— Terengganu (15 years of service)
🔧 Ministerial Reform Action: Establish dedicated rapid-response surge teams to protect routine primary care delivery!
